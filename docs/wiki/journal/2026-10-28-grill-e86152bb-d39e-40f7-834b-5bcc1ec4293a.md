## Question 1

```text
Round: Fungi icon: round 3, serious marks built on the pane
Question: Which direction should we refine?
Description (Markdown source):
Rebuilt from your description: Fungi is an **interaction engine**. It presents a window pane where skills author their own interface or workflow, for communication, alignment, feedback, and data collection. Every direction is a pane, kept restrained: graphite, paper, or indigo fields with at most one accent, and no characters or jokes.

- **Panes**: Three stacked glass panes on graphite. The front one is brought forward and lit, with a single signal-blue dot. *Fungi presents the pane; skills fill it.*
- **Frame**: Two heavy corners, one white and one signal blue, closing into a window. *Two parties meet and the interface forms between them.*
- **Dialog**: A dialog (title, field, button) floating over a dimmed workspace, in deep indigo. The most literal: *an interactive surface for feedback and data collection.*
- **Modules**: An **F** assembled from glossy interface panes on cream, with one pane in signal orange. *Skills compose the interface from parts.* The only lettermark.
- **Aperture**: A tall pane of cool light opening in a dark field. *A window opens to the person.* Quiet and cinematic.
- **Common ground**: A solid pane and an outlined pane; the area they share is signal blue. *Alignment: the common ground between agent and person.* Flat and precise.

Each card shows the full icon, then 40 / 24 / 16px on light, plus a circle crop and 24 / 16px on dark. Colors and accents are placeholders. Say in Notes if a shape is right but its treatment isn't.
Recommendation: Frame. It holds up best at 16px and in a circle avatar, and it says two parties meet to form a window in one shape. Risk: corner brackets also mean “crop” or “fullscreen” in many apps. If you'd rather have depth and material, take Panes.
```
