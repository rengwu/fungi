## Question 1

```text
Round: Fungi icon: round 8, from scratch in the icon.museum vein
Question: Which icon do you want to take further?
Description (Markdown source):
Everything earlier is dropped. [icon.museum](https://icon.museum/)'s best icons share one habit: **a single strong form rendered as a real material** (enamel, glass, ceramic, glossy plastic, flat ink), in studio light with one hero color and rarely a face. These six follow that.

- **Enamel f**: A serif italic **f** in glossy white enamel on a vermilion gradient. The only lettermark, drawn from Charter Black Italic for this spike; a final version should get a custom-drawn letter.
- **Glass fan**: Three translucent glass cards fanned from one pivot, blue, violet, and pink, glowing where they overlap. On near-black.
- **Puff**: A soft four-lobed cushion with a gloss on each lobe, violet to magenta to orange. On white.
- **Frost**: A thick frosted-glass tile, slightly tilted, with pink, cyan, and amber light glowing through. On vivid blue.
- **Drops**: Two black drops pulling apart like liquid. Flat and minimal, on white.
- **Print**: A cream ceramic disc with a fine radial print in warm brown, like a spore print. On forest green.

Each card shows the full icon, then 40 / 24 / 16px on light, plus a circle crop and 24 / 16px on dark. In Notes, say which material or form to push, or which two to combine.
Recommendation: Glass fan. It has the most icon.museum-grade craft (translucency, light where the cards overlap, a crisp rim) while staying a simple silhouette. It reads at 16px as a bright fan on black. If you want something quieter and more ownable, take Drops.
```
