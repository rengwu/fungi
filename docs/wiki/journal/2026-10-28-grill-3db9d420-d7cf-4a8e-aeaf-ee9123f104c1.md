## Question 1

```text
Round: Fungi icon: round 11, restarted from icon.museum
Question: Which icon do you want to take further?
Description (Markdown source):
Everything earlier is gone, kept designs included. I restudied all 254 icons on [icon.museum](https://icon.museum/), plus the 12 it features. Its strongest icons fall into a handful of archetypes: real hardware rendered as material, soft minimal gradients, polished chrome, retro screens, tactile controls, and papercraft. Each icon below takes a different one, drawn fresh, with nothing carried over from earlier rounds.

- **Bell**: **Hardware object**, like the gallery's cameras and turntables. A chrome service bell on a black base, on cobalt.
- **Ripple**: **Soft minimal gradient**, in the spirit of Zenith's sunrise. Concentric blue ripples spreading from a single drop, on warm grey.
- **Chrome**: **Polished chrome abstract**, in the spirit of Comet. A liquid-metal drop reflecting a horizon, on black.
- **Pixel**: **Retro screen**, like the gallery's LCD and dot-matrix icons. A small beige device whose green dot-matrix screen shows a pixel spark.
- **Button**: **Tactile control**, like its keys and switches. A big glossy red push button in a black housing, seen from above, on cream.
- **Strata**: **Papercraft**. Layered paper waves in sunrise colors, each casting a soft shadow, with a pale sun.

Each card shows the full icon, then 40 / 24 / 16px on light, plus a circle crop and 24 / 16px on dark. In Notes, say which archetype is closest, even if the drawing isn't.
Recommendation: Bell. It has the most convincing material (chrome on a black base), the most recognizable silhouette at every size, and it happens to fit what Fungi does: it rings the agent when the person is done. If you want something that feels more like a developer tool, take Pixel.
Options: Bell: A chrome service bell on a black base, on cobalt / Ripple: Soft concentric ripples from a single blue drop, on warm grey / Chrome: A liquid-metal drop reflecting a horizon, on black / Pixel: A small device whose dot-matrix screen glows with a pixel spark / Button: A big glossy red push button in a black housing, on cream / Strata: Layered paper waves in sunrise colours with a pale sun
Selected: Pixel: A small device whose dot-matrix screen glows with a pixel spark
Notes: go for pixel. enlarge the screen area to reduce the bezel thickness. remove the dots on the bezel
```
