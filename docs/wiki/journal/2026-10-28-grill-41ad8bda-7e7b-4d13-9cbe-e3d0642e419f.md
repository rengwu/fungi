## Question 1

```text
Round: Fungi icon: round 1 directions
Question: Which direction should we take further?
Description (Markdown source):
The reference ([appicons.store](https://www.appicons.store/)) uses squircle icons with saturated backgrounds, soft glossy 3D, and mostly simple mascots with two-dot faces. A few are flat neon/black marks or holographic glass. These six spikes cover that range, from mascot to symbol to letterform.

- **Sprout — glossy mascot**: Friendly 3D character, warm cap on teal. Closest to the reference store's mascot icons. Says *approachable*, not much about what Fungi does.
- **Mycelium — glowing network**: A glowing mushroom above the soil line with its network spreading below, each node a helper Fungi launches. Tells the product story: a small surface with hidden connective plumbing.
- **Prompt — flat CLI mark**: Flat black mushroom with a `>_` prompt cut into the cap, on neon lime. The clearest signal that this is a CLI, and it holds up at 16px.
- **Holo — iridescent glass**: Iridescent glass cap and frosted stem on black, with no face. Premium and quiet, though it says the least about the product.
- **Jelly — translucent spore buddy**: A translucent blue mascot with big eyes and floating spores on violet. Softer and more whimsical than Sprout, with a different material.
- **Monogram — mushroom “f”**: A lowercase **f** whose hook is a mushroom cap, in glossy black and coral on cream. Typographic, works as a wordmark companion.

Each card shows the full icon, then 40 / 24 / 16px on light and dark. Use Notes to combine directions (for example *Mycelium's network with Jelly's material*).
Recommendation: Mycelium. It is the only direction that says what Fungi does: a small surface over a hidden network that connects agents to helpers. It also pairs naturally with the name. Its roots soften to a glow below 32px, but the cap still reads. If tiny sizes matter most, Prompt is the safer pick.
Options: Sprout — glossy mascot / Mycelium — glowing network / Prompt — flat CLI mark / Holo — iridescent glass / Jelly — translucent spore buddy / Monogram — mushroom “f”
Selected: Something else
Notes: why is it all mushrooms? don't actually show a fungi. we don't have to make it actually related to the name. study what is this project for, and scrap and redesign all.
```
