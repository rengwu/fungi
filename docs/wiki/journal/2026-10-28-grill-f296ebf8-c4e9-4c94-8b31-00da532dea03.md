## Question 1

```text
Round: Fungi icon: round 5, back in the appicons.store vein
Question: Which direction should we refine?
Description (Markdown source):
I restudied [appicons.store](https://www.appicons.store/). Its consistent vein is chunky, soft objects filling most of the tile, rendered in **pearly iridescence, frosted glass, or glossy jelly**, on deep navy, violet, vivid blue, or clean white. Life comes from **minimal pill eyes**, with no mouths, cheeks, or props. Several of its icons are already panes or screens.

Each direction keeps the brief so far: a **pane** (Fungi presents an interface), a **hint of a living organism**, and restraint over gags.

- **Pearl**: A pillowy window with a title-bar groove and two pill eyes, in pearl white with orange, pink, violet, and cyan bleeding in from the edges. On near-white.
- **Reach**: The same pearly material, but the pane stretches a pseudopod from one corner. It reads as alive and as a speech bubble. Eyes cut in navy, on deep navy.
- **Spore**: No face. A thick frosted-glass frame on violet with a glowing mint-cyan cell inside. The most serious of the six.
- **Screen**: A soft periwinkle device whose dark screen shows two glowing mint pill eyes. The pane itself is the creature.
- **Jelly**: No face. A glossy pink-to-blue jelly pane mid-division, budding a smaller pane, on vivid blue.
- **Ink**: A glossy black pane with white pill eyes, budding a small pane from its lower corner. It also reads as a speech bubble. Monochrome, on white.

Each card shows the full icon, then 40 / 24 / 16px on light, plus a circle crop and 24 / 16px on dark. Say in Notes if you want one direction's shape in another's material, or no eyes at all.
Recommendation: Reach. It's closest to the reference's polish and carries all three ideas at once: a pane, alive (the pseudopod), and communicating (it reads as a speech bubble). The pill eyes keep it cool rather than cute, and the white shape on navy holds up at 16px. If you want no face, take Spore.
```
