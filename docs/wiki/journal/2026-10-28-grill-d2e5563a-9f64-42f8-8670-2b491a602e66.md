## Question 1

```text
Round: Fungi icon: round 7, Pebble and Pearl eyes
Question: Which eyes?
Description (Markdown source):
The same Pebble (navy) and Pearl (white) bodies; only the eyes change. Each card shows both, then 32 / 16px on light and dark.

- **Pills (current)**: Round 6 as you saw it: tall, calm, the reference's default.
- **Small dots**: Two small round dots, set wider. Quieter and more minimal.
- **Glossy round**: Bigger round eyes with catchlights. The most expressive; closest to the store's 3D characters.
- **Side glance**: White eyes with outlines and pupils looking up and to the side. Curious, a little comic.
- **Happy arcs**: Closed, smiling eyes. Content and friendly.
- **Half-lidded**: Half-lidded: the lower half of each eye only. Deadpan and cool; unusual in the reference set.
- **Single eye**: One big glossy eye. The most graphic and unusual.
- **Embossed**: No ink: the eyes are carved into the pearl, with a light catch on the lower rim. The most restrained.

In Notes, say whether to adjust the size, spacing, height, or color of your pick, or to mix two treatments (for example, half-lidded but embossed).
Recommendation: Half-lidded. It gives the pearl a cool, deadpan personality that none of the reference icons has, so it's the most memorable, and it still reads as two eyes at 16px. If you want it quieter, take Embossed.
```
