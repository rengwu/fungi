## Question 2

```text
Round: Fungi icon: round 2, redesigned from what Fungi does
Question: Which drawing should we take further?
Description (Markdown source):
- **Boomerang**: A glossy boomerang in flight with a dotted path curving back. **Round trip**: Fungi sends work out to a person and it comes back to the agent.
- **Mailbox**: A rural mailbox with its flag up. **The flag tells you**: the answer lands in the box and the agent doesn't keep checking. That's the no-polling promise.
- **Doze**: A sleeping speech-bubble character with a notification badge. **Sleeps until pinged**: the agent spends nothing while you take your time, then gets woken.
- **Socket**: A flat outlet that doubles as a surprised face, on neon lime. **Headless**: Fungi has no UI of its own, and any interface plugs in.
- **Resume**: A dimmed pause bar beside a glowing play triangle. **Pause, then play**: the agent pauses, the person acts, the agent resumes.
- **Receipt**: A paper receipt with a check stamp. **Nothing is lost**: every answer is saved before it's acknowledged, and you get a receipt.

Each card shows the full icon, then 40 / 24 / 16px on light, plus a circle crop and 24 / 16px on dark. Use Notes to mix ideas, e.g. *Doze's character with Socket's flat lime treatment*. A flat one-color version for favicons comes after we pick a direction.
Recommendation: Doze. It carries the sleeps-until-pinged idea and has the most character, in line with the reference. Risk: below 24px it can pass for a chat app's notification icon. If that bothers you, take Mailbox for the same idea or Resume for a pure symbol.
Options: Boomerang: round trip / Mailbox: flag goes up when it's done / Doze: sleeps until pinged / Socket: any interface plugs in / Resume: pause, then play / Receipt: nothing is lost
```
